Bubbo Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play Bubbo, a balloon survival game in which you avoid enemies and hazards, collect stars, try to beat your high score, and unlock enemy types in the Almanac.

1. Information We Collect

Gameplay Information

Bubbo does not require an account or registration. High scores and Almanac progress may be stored locally on your device. The game does not provide cloud saves through the developer.

Advertising Information

Bubbo uses Google AdMob to display interstitial advertisements. Rewarded advertisements are not currently part of the game.

When advertisements are requested or displayed, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and its service providers may collect or process information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • App interactions, such as app launches, ad impressions, and interactions with advertisements;
  • Diagnostics and technical information used to assess performance, stability, and errors;
  • Device or other identifiers used for advertising, measurement, security, and fraud prevention.

The Android Release manifest for Bubbo does not request the Android Advertising ID permission. This does not necessarily mean that the advertising SDK cannot process other device identifiers or technical information.

2. How Information Is Used

Advertising-related information may be used to:

  • Provide and display advertisements;
  • Measure advertisement impressions and performance;
  • Understand interactions with advertisements;
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve reliability;
  • Detect invalid advertising traffic, fraud, and abuse;
  • Maintain the security and proper operation of advertising services.

The developer does not operate a separate analytics or crash-reporting service as part of Bubbo's own gameplay code. However, third-party SDKs may process technical and interaction data as described in their own policies.

3. How Information Is Shared

Gameplay progress stored locally by Bubbo is not uploaded to a developer-operated cloud save service. Information processed by Google AdMob may be transmitted to Google and its relevant service providers to provide advertising and related services.

For more information about Google's data practices, please read the Google Privacy Policy .

4. Data Security

Google states that data transmitted by its Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

High scores and Almanac progress stored locally may remain on your device until the app's data is cleared or the app is uninstalled, depending on your device and Android settings.

Bubbo does not provide a developer-operated account or account-based data deletion service. Advertising-related information handled by Google is subject to Google's retention, deletion, and privacy practices.

6. Children's Privacy and Intended Audience

Bubbo's current Google Play target audience declaration includes users aged 13 and older. The game does not require account registration. Advertising services may nevertheless process device, technical, and advertising-related information as described in this policy.

If the app's target audience or advertising configuration changes, this policy and the applicable store declarations should be reviewed and updated accordingly.

7. Third-Party Services

Bubbo uses Google AdMob for advertising. Google's handling of information is governed by its own privacy policy and applicable service terms. The developer does not control Google's independent data-handling practices.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the game's features, advertising setup, or applicable requirements change. The revised policy will be published at this page, with an updated effective date where appropriate.

9. Contact

For questions about Bubbo or this Privacy Policy, please use the developer contact information provided on Bubbo's Google Play store listing.
