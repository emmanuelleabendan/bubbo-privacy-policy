Bubbo! Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you play Bubbo!, a balloon survival game in which you avoid enemies and hazards, collect stars, try to beat your high score, and unlock enemy types in the Almanac.

1. Information Stored by the Game

Bubbo! does not require an account or registration. Gameplay progress, including high scores, settings, currency, and Almanac progress, may be stored locally on your device. The developer does not provide a cloud-save service for this gameplay data.

Local data may remain on your device until you clear the app's data, uninstall the game, or otherwise remove it using your device's settings. Exact behavior may depend on your device and Android configuration.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Data

Bubbo! uses Google AdMob to display interstitial and rewarded advertisements. Rewarded advertisements allow players to earn 50 Bubblies, the game's virtual currency, after the ad's reward conditions are met.

When ads are requested or displayed, Google Mobile Ads SDK and its service providers may collect or process information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • App interactions, including ad requests, impressions, and interactions with ads;
  • Diagnostic, performance, and crash-related information, where applicable;
  • Device or other identifiers used for advertising, measurement, security, and fraud prevention.

The information processed can depend on the SDK version, device and app configuration, consent choices, age-related settings, and advertising services provided. The developer does not directly receive all information that third-party services may process.

The Android Advertising ID permission setting in a particular build does not, by itself, establish that no other device identifiers or technical information are processed. Advertising behavior and disclosures should be verified against the actual release build and applicable SDK documentation.

3. How Information May Be Used

Advertising-related information may be processed to:

  • Request, deliver, and display advertisements;
  • Measure ad impressions, performance, and interactions;
  • Provide reporting and service functionality;
  • Diagnose technical issues and improve reliability;
  • Detect invalid traffic, fraud, and abuse; and
  • Maintain the security and proper operation of advertising services.

4. Sharing of Information

Gameplay progress stored locally by Bubbo! is not uploaded to a developer-operated cloud-save service. Information processed by Google AdMob may be transmitted to Google and relevant service providers to deliver ads and provide related services, subject to Google's terms, privacy practices, and applicable settings.

For more information, see the Google Privacy Policy and Google's AdMob data disclosure guidance.

5. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where applicable, Bubbo! may use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain and manage advertising-related consent choices. The consent options shown to a player may depend on location, applicable requirements, and the consent configuration in the app.

Available privacy choices and settings may also depend on the platform, Google's services, and the app's current implementation. The actual consent flow and any privacy-options entry point should be checked in the released app before publication of this policy.

6. Children's Privacy and Audience

Bubbo!'s Google Play target audience declaration includes users aged 13–15, 16–17, and 18 and older. The game does not require account registration, but third-party advertising services may still process device, technical, and advertising-related information as described in this policy.

The game's content rating and its declared target audience are separate store settings. Advertising and data processing must be configured to meet the requirements that apply to the declared audience, including any age-related restrictions, consent requirements, and applicable Google Play Families Policy requirements. This policy does not replace those technical or store-compliance requirements.

7. Data Security and Retention

Google states that data transmitted by its services is protected in transit where supported by the service. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Local gameplay data may remain on the device until it is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Advertising-related information handled by Google and its service providers is subject to their own retention, deletion, and privacy practices.

8. Third-Party Services

Bubbo! uses Google AdMob and may use Google's UMP consent tools. Google's handling of information is governed by its own policies and applicable service terms. The developer does not control Google's independent data-handling practices.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the game's features, advertising configuration, third-party services, or applicable requirements change. The revised policy will be published at the same location, with an updated effective date where appropriate.

10. Contact

For questions about Bubbo! or this Privacy Policy, contact the developer using the contact information provided on Bubbo!'s Google Play store listing.
