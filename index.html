Bubbo Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

Bubbo is a mobile game published by TenPoint7. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you play Bubbo.

Bubbo is intended for children. We aim to protect children's privacy and configure the game and its advertising services in accordance with applicable privacy laws and Google Play Families requirements.

1. Information Stored by the Game

Bubbo does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to play.

The game stores gameplay information locally on the device as needed to operate its features. Bubbo does not use its own analytics service, crash-reporting service, cloud-save system, or account system.

Information processed by third-party services, including advertising services, is described below.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Data Processing

Bubbo uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including optional rewarded advertisements that may provide in-game rewards.

Depending on the SDK, configuration, and applicable settings, Google and its advertising services may process:

  • Device and app information
  • Advertising identifiers or related signals, where available and permitted
  • Ad impressions and interactions
  • Information used for advertising delivery and measurement
  • Diagnostic, security, and fraud-prevention information

The information processed may vary. We do not control all data-processing practices of third-party providers.

Bubbo is configured for child-directed advertising, with a maximum ad content rating of G. The game is also configured to remove the relevant Android advertising ID permissions from its Release manifest. These settings do not necessarily mean that no information is processed by Google or that every identifier or data-processing mechanism is disabled.

Google's processing of information is subject to its applicable policies and settings.

3. How Information Is Used

Gameplay information stored locally is used to operate the game and maintain its gameplay features.

Information processed by Google AdMob may be used, as applicable, to:

  • Deliver and display advertisements
  • Provide optional rewarded-ad functionality
  • Measure advertising performance
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Diagnose technical issues and maintain service security

We do not operate a separate analytics or advertising-data collection system within Bubbo.

4. Sharing of Information

Bubbo does not independently sell players' personal information.

Information may be processed by Google and relevant service providers to deliver advertising and related services. These providers may process information under their own policies and applicable legal obligations.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, or security.

5. Data Retention and Security

Gameplay information stored locally remains on the device until it is removed by the user, the operating system, or another applicable device or app-management process.

Retention of information processed by Google AdMob depends on Google's services, policies, and settings. We do not control all third-party retention practices.

We take reasonable steps to protect information under our control. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy and Parental Questions

Bubbo is intended for children, and its advertising configuration is designed to support child-directed use.

The game does not intentionally ask children to submit personal information directly through the game. However, third-party advertising services may process information as described in this policy.

Parents or guardians who have questions or concerns about Bubbo's privacy practices may contact the developer using the details in Section 8.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Bubbo's features, third-party services, data practices, or applicable legal requirements change.

The latest version will be available at this webpage. The effective date will be updated when material changes are made.
